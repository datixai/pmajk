# Brief

- **Project:** complete redesign of the website of the Prime Minister of Azad Jammu & Kashmir (ajkpm.gok.pk), to pitch to the Government of AJK. Local test first.
- **Reference:** https://ajkpm.gok.pk/ (the client's own site) and the family look of the AJK-Gov-Redesign project (pine green, saffron and chinar red; Fraunces, Plus Jakarta Sans and Noto Nastaliq Urdu).
- **Keep:** all existing information, photos and details: the Prime Minister's profile, mission and vision, cabinet, organogram, political wing, former Prime Ministers, press releases, notifications, gallery, AJK at a Glance and contact details.
- **Fix:** remove injected spam, fix broken links and images, correct wrong information, finish half-built parts. Every problem and fix is listed in `docs/PITCH_REPORT.md`.
- **Design:** the aura and dignity of the Prime Minister's office; a premium, stately, centred layout with a strong portrait hero; full-HD images only; chinar leaf favicon; small AJK and Pakistan flags in the footer; "Developed by Datix AI" credit.
- **Identity:** AJK as the base camp of the freedom struggle for Indian Illegally Occupied Jammu & Kashmir.
- **Add:** an admin panel where staff can add, edit, reorder, upload and delete every kind of content.
- **Security:** top level, as for a government site that hostile attackers may target.
- **Tech:** Next.js 16.4, React 19, Tailwind CSS 4, file-based content store.
